# Privacy Policy — Goal Challenge

Effective date: [DATE]

Goal Challenge is an offline game. The app, as supplied in this source package, does not create user accounts, access location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos/files, or transmit gameplay data to a server. Score and game settings are stored locally on the device using Android SharedPreferences and may participate in the user's Android backup/device-transfer settings.

The app may use the device Text-to-Speech engine to announce the round winner and the device vibration service for optional haptic feedback. These features do not require the app to collect personal information.

If future versions add analytics, advertising, cloud saves, account systems, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs, this privacy policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before release.
